## Source and attribution coverage

[data/sources.jsonl](data/sources.jsonl) and [source notes](docs/source-notes.md) identify selected pinned repositories, inspected file hashes, license references and notices found in those license documents. A notice copied from a license document is not necessarily a complete list of source-code contributors or copyright holders. File-specific notices and exceptions may also apply.

The historical export does not provide complete repository/revision/license provenance for all snippets. **Absent source entries and unchecked licenses are unknown**, not public domain or implicitly MIT. Selected later checks do not attest the source checkout used by the historical analysis. Do not present the entire corpus as cleared for unrestricted code reuse.

The dataset includes nonverbatim report descriptions and redacted excerpts as well as code anchors. Their status must not be silently converted into an exact quotation. No complete upstream repository is bundled; linked upstream material retains its own terms.

Historical preservation is a research convention, not a promise to retain material against a valid rights or privacy request. Necessary removals should be documented without republishing the removed sensitive material.
